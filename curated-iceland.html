Your week in Iceland

Days 1–2

Reykjavik & the Blue Lagoon

Arrive in Keflavik, settle into Reykjavik, and unwind in the geothermal waters of the Reykjanes Peninsula.

Days 3–4

Golden Circle & South Coast

See Thingvellir, Geysir, and Gullfoss before travelling past black-sand beaches and coastal waterfalls.

Days 5–6

Glacier lagoon & aurora watch

Explore Jokulsarlon and Diamond Beach, then join an expert-led aurora outing when the sky is clear.

Day 7

A gentle return to Reykjavik

Enjoy a relaxed morning and transfer back to Keflavik for your return flight.
