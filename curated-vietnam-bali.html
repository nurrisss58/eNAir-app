From Vietnam to Bali

Days 1–3

Hanoi & the Old Quarter

Settle into the capital, explore its historic streets, and sample local dishes with a neighbourhood guide.

Days 4–6

Ha Long Bay

Spend two nights among limestone karsts, with time for a quiet sunrise paddle and a visit to a floating village.

Days 7–9

Central Vietnam

Connect south to Hoi An for lantern-lit evenings, a cooking class, and a slow day by the coast.

Days 10–14

Bali, at your own pace

Finish in Bali with a choice of Ubud walks, temple visits, spa time, and a final night near the coast.
