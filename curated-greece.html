Mediterranean Escape

Begin among the landmarks and neighbourhood tables of Athens, then follow the Aegean to island harbours, clear water, and sunlit evenings.

Best seasonMay to October for warm days and island ferries

Ten days around the Aegean

Days 1–3

Athens & the Acropolis

Explore the Acropolis Museum, walk the old Plaka lanes, and make time for a relaxed rooftop dinner.

Days 4–5

Nafplio & the Peloponnese

Travel to Nafplio for a harbour-side stay, Venetian architecture, and an easy day trip to ancient sites.

Days 6–8

Island days in the Cyclades

Take the ferry to a Cycladic island for swimming coves, village walks, and a sunset sail.

Days 9–10

Return to Athens

Enjoy a final island morning, return to the capital, and leave time for a last meal before departure.
